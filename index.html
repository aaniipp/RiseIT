Mentoring Tracks

Available to students from all universities across Indonesia.

IT Advisory

This module introduces students to the strategic side of consulting, understanding client challenges, analyzing business needs, and designing IT transformation roadmaps that align technology with business goals.

Focus Areas: Digital strategy, system optimization, IT project management

IT Implementation

This module focuses on executing digital transformation initiatives, turning strategy into action through system integration, software implementation, and digital tools that drive measurable results.

Focus Areas: System integration, software implementation, digital tools

IT Assurance

This module develops the evaluation and governance mindset, ensuring IT initiatives deliver value, manage risk, and sustain long-term performance through auditing and continuous improvement.

Focus Areas: IT audit, IT governance, risk management
